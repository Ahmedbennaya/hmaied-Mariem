— 01 / About

About Mariem
Hmaied

Mariem Hmaied creates functional, precise, and client-focused interiors for homes, dressing spaces, kitchens, and custom layouts.

A graduate of Collège LaSalle Tunis (BTP, Décoration d'Intérieur, 2016), Mariem has worked across architecture offices, joinery workshops, kitchen and aluminium showrooms, and since October 2022 as a freelance designer with JK Building. Her work covers sketches, APS and APD, technical drawings, realistic 3D renderings, material samples, supplier coordination, and site follow-up.

— 05 / Process

How a project
moves forward.

The workflow follows Mariem's CV experience: active listening, design studies, detailed drawings, coordinated suppliers, and site control.

01
Listen & Measure
Clarify the client's needs, preferences, budget, safety constraints, and site measurements before any drawing starts.
↗
02
Sketches APS / APD
Build the first layout direction with sketches, mood references, proportions, materials, and clear project options.
↗
03
Plans & 3D
Prepare detailed technical drawings, KitchenDraw or 2020 Design views, and realistic renderings for client validation.
↗
04
Suppliers & Quotes
Coordinate materials, suppliers, entrepreneurs, commercial offers, and practical solutions for technical difficulties.
↗
05
Chantier Follow-up
Track the work on site, technical prescriptions, timelines, costs, quality of materials, and delivery until reception.
